# Note Nerds privacy policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Note Nerds is a local-first notebook and drawing application. This policy explains how the version distributed through Apple’s App Store handles information.

## Information collection

The developer does not collect personal information, notebook content, drawings, imported files, search terms, usage data, advertising identifiers, or analytics from Note Nerds.

The app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, or tracking system.

## Local storage

Notebooks, drawings, text, imported files, settings, and search data are stored on the user’s device. Deleting the app may delete local data that has not been exported or synchronized.

## iCloud

Note Nerds can use Apple CloudKit to keep notebook data in the user’s private iCloud account. Apple provides and operates iCloud under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive or sell the user’s private CloudKit content.

Users can control iCloud access through Apple ID and iCloud settings on their device.

## Imported and exported files

Users choose the PDFs and images they import. They also choose where exported notebooks, PDFs, and images are sent through Apple’s document picker and share sheet. Note Nerds does not send these files to a developer-operated server.

## Children

Note Nerds does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The app is not directed to the Kids category and contains no advertising or public communication feature.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a release that changes data handling. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Note Nerds support page:

`https://github.com/CoolAssPuppy/notenerds/issues`

Do not include private notebook content or other sensitive information in a public issue.
